Add an Antigravity account

  1. Open Google sign-in and sign in with the account you want to add.
  2. Google then shows an authorization code. Copy it and paste it here.

Remove account?

This removes the credential from CLIProxyAPI but keeps the agy home and its sign-in. If this account is imported automatically, it can return after reconfiguration. Disable it instead to keep it out of rotation.
How multiple accounts work

Each account has its own sign-in, settings, conversations, and usage limits. New chats follow CLIProxyAPI’s routing strategy. By default, round-robin spreads requests across eligible accounts of the same priority; it does not split token usage equally.

When your chat client sends back a conversation ID, that chat stays on its original account. A client can continue it on another account by starting a new session with the chat history or a summary. This page manages accounts, not chats.
