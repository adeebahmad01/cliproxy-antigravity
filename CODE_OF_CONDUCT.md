# Code of Conduct

This project aims to maintain a technical, constructive, and harassment-free collaboration space.

Contributors are expected to discuss ideas and code on their merits, communicate respectfully, avoid personal attacks or discriminatory language, and accept maintainers' decisions about scope and safety. Deliberate harassment, threats, doxxing, spam, or repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

For conduct concerns that should not be handled publicly, contact the maintainer through the private contact options available on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
